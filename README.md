# Tata-data-visualisation-dashboard
Designed and developed an interactive Executive Retail Sales Dashboard in Microsoft Power Bl as part of the Tata Data Visualisation Virtual Experience Program on Forage.
Key Highlights:
* Cleaned and transformed retail transaction data by removing invalid records (negative quantities and incorrect unit prices).
* Created DAX measures to calculate Total Revenue (Quantity × Unit Price) and other key business metrics.
* Built interactive KPI cards to track Total Revenue, Quantity Sold, Customer Count, and Countries Served.
* Analyzed monthly revenue trends to identify seasonality and business performance in 2011.
* Identified the Top 10 countries and Top 10 customers based on revenue generation.
* Developed a geographic map visualization to analyze global product demand while excluding the United Kingdom.
* Designed an executive dashboard with business-focused insights to support strategic decision-making.
Tools & Technologies:
* Microsoft Power BI
* DAX
* Power Query
* Data Cleaning
* Data Visualization
* Dashboard Design
* Business Analytics
